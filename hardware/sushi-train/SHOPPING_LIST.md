### Option B: Dedicated 3D Printing & Maker Specialists
*Best if you prefer supporting dedicated open-source 3D printing vendors and want official Prusa / high-end maker parts.*

1. **[Printed Solid](https://www.printedsolid.com/) (Newark, DE - Official Prusa US Partner):**
   * **Best For:** **Prusament PETG** (the exact filament tuned for your Core One+), genuine BigTreeTech drivers, Stepperonline motors, and heat-set inserts.
   * Ships very quickly from the East Coast US.
2. **[Adafruit](https://www.adafruit.com/) (New York, NY):**
   * **Best For:** High-grade ESP32 boards (like the Adafruit ESP32 Feather or QT Py ESP32-S3), breadboards, jumper wire packs, and guaranteed genuine electronics with industry-standard documentation.
3. **[Filastruder](https://www.filastruder.com/) (Georgia, US):**
   * **Best For:** Authentic BigTreeTech TMC2209 silent stepper drivers, genuine LDO and Stepperonline motors, and high-quality 3D printer hardware.
4. **[STEPPERONLINE Direct](https://www.omc-stepperonline.com/):**
   * Direct manufacturer warehouse pricing if ordering stepper motors in bulk or specific D-shaft lengths.

### Option C: Local Walk-In (Get Started Today!)

If you want to pick up parts immediately:

1. **[Micro Center](https://www.microcenter.com/) (If you live within driving distance):**
   * **Maker / DIY Aisle:** Stocks ESP32 dev boards, mini breadboards, jumper wires, TMC2209 drivers, NEMA 17 steppers, and power supplies on the shelf.
   * **3D Printing Aisle:** Sells **Inland PETG** (high quality, made by eSUN/Polymaker) for $17–$19/spool.
2. **Home Depot / Lowe's / Ace Hardware:**
   * **Hardware Aisle:** Buy individual **M8 x 30mm hex bolt**, **M8 nyloc nut**, and washers for ~$1.50 without having to buy a 50-pack online.
   * **Paint & Lubricant Aisle:** Stocks **WD-40 Specialist Dry Lube with PTFE** on the shelf.
   * **Cabinet Hardware:** Clear silicone cabinet bumper feet.
